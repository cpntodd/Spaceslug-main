# Development log

This index links chronological, evidence-based progress reports. Entries distinguish planned work from implemented and validated work.

## Entry format

Each entry should include:

- date and status;
- Spaceslug-main and `vulkan-runtime` revisions;
- objective and exact change;
- environment and hardware;
- commands used;
- before/after measurements with units and methodology;
- artifacts, datasets, and experiment IDs;
- limitations, failures, and follow-up work.

A devlog entry must not turn a design proposal into a claim of working behavior. The machine-readable schema, manifest, and experiment record are authoritative.
